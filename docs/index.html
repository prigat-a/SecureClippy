Small, private, out of your way

Everything you need from a clipboard manager, nothing that phones home.

⌨️

Menu bar + ⌘⇧V

Click the menu bar icon, or press ⌘⇧V and the same menu drops down at your pointer. Click an entry or press its number to paste it into the app you're using.

👆

Touch ID lock

History stays locked until you unlock it with Touch ID or your Mac password. It locks again when your screen locks or the Mac sleeps.

🔐

Encrypted in memory

Each entry is sealed with AES-256-GCM. Nothing is written to disk, and quitting the app throws the key away.

🙈

Skips your passwords

Items that password managers like 1Password and Bitwarden mark as secret are never recorded.

🧹

Cleans up after itself

Entries expire after 24 hours, and the history keeps at most 200 items.

🪶

Featherweight

A native Apple Silicon app under 200 KB. No window, no Dock icon, about 0% CPU while idle.

Install

About a minute. You only do steps 3 and 4 once.

  1. Download and unzip

    Download SecureClipboard.zip. Safari unzips it for you; otherwise double-click the zip.

  2. Move it to Applications

    Drag Secure Clipboard from Downloads into your Applications folder.

  3. Open it the first time

    The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS warns you the first time. Double-click it, click Done on the warning, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

    Once it's running, look for the clipboard icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen. There's no window or Dock icon.

    On macOS 14 or earlier, or prefer Terminal?

    On macOS 12–14: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

    Or remove the download flag in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Secure Clipboard.app"

  4. Allow pasting (optional)

    To paste straight into other apps, turn on Secure Clipboard in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. macOS offers to take you there the first time you paste. Without it, picking an entry just copies it and you press ⌘V yourself.

    After updating to a new version, turn it off and on again (or remove and re-add the app) in that list.

Want it to start with your Mac? System Settings → General → Login Items → add Secure Clipboard.
